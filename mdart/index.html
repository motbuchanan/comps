About

Michael DeLisi

I texture and look-dev creatures and hard-surface assets: skin, scale, chipped paint, rust and everything light does when it hits them.

I studied Visual Effects at the Savannah College of Art and Design and graduated in 2022. Since then I have kept building my eye through mentorships and courses, working on models from artists like Kurtis Dawe.

I sculpt in ZBrush and still draw with a pencil. I am looking for texture and look development work on film, series and game cinematics.
